The new model

A sebutharga owns its prices

Before, one undated price list served every project. Now each sebutharga owns versioned rate books, and a project’s prices come from the sebutharga it was issued under.

The discovery report found one global master BQ with no year, no contract and no history, and a sebutharga number that was only a text label. v2 keeps the project workflow and the money formulas, and replaces what sits under them.

What changed

The price source. A contract is a record with a status, a panel and a stack of rate-book versions.

What did not

The stack (React, Hono, D1), the six project statuses and the money formulas. A parity run found 0 differences.

What it costs

A lock and a preview step: a project with a No. Inden no longer swaps freely, and a swap is never one click.

The decisions that shaped it

You settled eight decisions before any code was written (CONTRACT.md §0). The first four define the model; the last four say how it is built. Builders were told not to reopen them.

1 · The sebutharga decides the prices

Every company on its panel uses its kadar. A standalone sebutharga has its own rates and a small panel, often one project.

2 · No year-binding

A sebutharga lives on its own and can start any day. The financial year only owns votes and allocations.

3 · A new project takes the AKTIF one

A PJA may swap it. Any officer may create a sebutharga by uploading its master BQ Excel.

4 · History like git commits

Every upload, publish, activation and swap is logged with who, when and a diff. Draf projects whose items changed get an indicator.

  • 5 · Re-price, don’t rebuild. Old items map to new ones, with a preview of old against new per row.
  • 6 · Excel import is hybrid. A script reads every rate and unit from the cell; fuzzy matching only suggests, and a human approves.
  • 7 · No hard-coding. Profile, logo, lists, policy rules with effective dates and printed texts are editable.
  • 8 · Design is Direction C “Tapak”, light mode only, no dark mode anywhere.

Spec note: the build spec says “the 4 owner decisions”; CONTRACT.md lists eight, so all eight are shown.

Why “lock at No. Inden”?

By the time a No. Inden is issued, money is committed to a company at a price. A swap after that would change a figure someone already acted on. Unlocking stays possible, but it is an explicit act and it is logged. Needs your answer Should only PT or Admin unlock? (chapter 90, p. {{page:90}})
