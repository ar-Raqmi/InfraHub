What exists now

  • A sebutharga is a record that owns versioned rate books, with an Excel import (ch. 07).
  • Every project is bound to one; swap and re-price with a preview (ch. 06).
  • Tetapan Organisasi (ch. 08), a light-only look (ch. 05), a dashboard with report scope (ch. 09).

What is proven

  • Every builder spec passes on the live preview, not only on a laptop.
  • The real master BQ Excel parses with 0 warnings: 465 of 465 priced rows.
  • Production was never touched: the preview is a separate project, database and bucket.

What nothing has checked

  • Real iPhone and Android rendering.
  • An officer reading the 154 reworded match suggestions.
  • Go-live: no production migration was run, by design.

What needs your decision

Seven main questions, each with a default already chosen

Nothing is blocked: the lead picked defaults. Start with three: which date decides the LAD rate, which Excel book is the 2026 one, and who may unlock a project that already has a No. Inden.

The rest: keep today’s wording mistakes, keep the RM 200k cap as a warning, keep AI-assisted import off, plan the go-live. Chapter 90, p. {{page:90}} lists them with seven smaller builder questions.

In one paragraph

InfraHub v2 turns a sebutharga from a text label into a real record. It owns its prices, lives apart from the calendar year, keeps every version like a git history, and tells an officer before it changes a draf project. A lead and six builders wrote it in two waves on 21 to 22 November 2026 (about two hours of clock time), and the lead merged and re-tested it. Every builder spec also passes on a live, isolated preview. What is left is yours: seven main questions, seven smaller ones and the go-live plan.

Emerald, teal InfraHub itself: code, tables, statuses, builders.
Amber A risk, a refusal or a stale rate.
Slate Secondary information.

About the numbers

  • Times, costs and the merged-code results come from LEAD-FACTS.md, which the lead verified by running the commands. Each builder’s own numbers come from its REPORT file.
  • Cost is Hermes’ estimated API list price. The work ran on the Claude plan, which is billed in plan quota, not dollars (chapter 12, p. {{page:12}}).
  • Every screenshot shows the anonymised seed: “Pentadbir 01”, “PJA 01”, “Syarikat 01…63”. The repo is public.
  • Malay screen terms stay as they are, with a short gloss the first time: Sebutharga (quotation), Buku Kadar (rate book), Perlu harga semula (needs re-pricing).

The one idea

A sebutharga decides the prices. A project’s rates come from the sebutharga it was issued under, never from today’s date. Everything built here serves that sentence.
