What it built

  • Fonts. Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for the body: self-hosted (224 KB, OFL-1.1), with tabular figures for money.
  • Tokens. CSS variables in theme.css, mirrored in tw-config.js. color-scheme: light; no dark: class anywhere.
  • 17 primitives in components/ui, with typed props and no data fetching (page 2 of this chapter).
  • Shell. AppShell, Sidebar and BottomSheet re-skinned. Props and routes are unchanged.
  • Gallery. kit.html shows every primitive; DESIGN.md holds the rules.

Why “Tapak” and why light only

“Tapak” means site: Direction C is field-first, with touch targets of at least 44 px, 16 px inputs on a phone and no hover-only actions. The concept’s desktop screens were dark; you chose light only, so they were redrawn. Purple appears only on something that is a contract.
