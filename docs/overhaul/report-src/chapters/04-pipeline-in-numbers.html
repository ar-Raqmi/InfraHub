The journey of a finished project · n = 293 SIAP projects

Site work is only a third of the journey

From opening the file to Pemadanan takes a median of 118 days. The contractor is on site for 38 of them. The longest stretch comes after the site is finished: 62 days of claim, hand-over to Kewangan and matching. Verified

Before the site · 16 days

8 days from opening to lantikan, 4 to print the BPP, 3 to contract start (always BPP + 2 working days). Hand-over follows on the same day in the median case.

On site · 38 days

Every contract is exactly 56 days (8 weeks). 252 of 293 finished before the end date, 19 on the day and 22 after it, so LAD is rare.

After the site · 62 days

20 days to lodge the claim, 33 to reach Kewangan, 4 to match. The Dashboard only warns about contract-end notices; this wait has no owner and no reminder.
