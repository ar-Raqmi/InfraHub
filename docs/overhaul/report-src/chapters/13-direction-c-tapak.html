UI/UX direction C · field-first

Tapak

The PJA standing at a drain in the sun is the primary user. The phone becomes a short run of big single-purpose steps; the desk becomes a command centre for the PT.

The idea

Today’s sites, measure, photo, confirm: one thumb, a keypad and a visible “saved on the phone, sent when there is signal” state. A rate-book change reaches the PJA as a bottom sheet with two big choices. The desk shows live field activity per PJA on a time track and a queue of decisions for the PT.

Type and colour

Phone: a light, high-contrast “siang” palette with lime #B7F34D for the one primary action and amber for rate warnings. Desk: a dark “malam” palette (#0B1513) so the command centre reads as a different tool from the field app.

What to look at

  1. The contract chip on the phone project page, above the 6-step stepper.
  2. The “kadar berubah” bottom sheet: contract rate against new rate, two large choices, “PT akan sahkan”.
  3. The desk contract timeline with the version history and the Klon kontrak form.

Pros

  • Biggest change for the largest group: 12 of the 16 local accounts are PJA. Measuring stops being a desktop form on a phone.
  • Splits decisions cleanly: the PJA proposes, the PT approves, every rate choice is logged.
  • The command centre gives the PT live awareness without opening projects.

Cons

  • Real offline support (service worker, queue, conflicts) is a big engineering step; the mockup shows only the experience.
  • Two visual systems to maintain: light field app, dark desk app.
  • The desk BQ editing is not reinvented: the PT still needs B-style tables for checking.

Serves best

PJA site officers on phones; the PT gets an at-a-glance view of what came in from site.

Effort · Yiyi’s estimate, not measured

About 8–11 developer-weeks: phone flows 4–5, offline queue 2–3, command centre and contract timeline 2–3. Highest effort, highest impact for the PJA.

Answers audit issues

11, 12 (a real phone flow, short navigation), 1, 2 (contract chip, rate-change sheet) and 4 (contract timeline).
