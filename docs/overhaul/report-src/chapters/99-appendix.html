Appendix

Method, privacy and sources

How the numbers were produced, how sure each kind of statement is, what was kept out of this report on purpose, and where to find every rule in the code.

Method

  • Data. A full snapshot of the live D1 database, taken 20 Nov 2026 06:46 (+08), imported into a local copy. All queries ran read-only on that copy with wrangler d1 execute --local; production was never queried.
  • Code. Read on branch overhaul/discovery-2026-11; no repository file was edited.
  • Counting rules. A project’s year is the calendar year of Tarikh Buka, as the app does. Days are calendar days. “Item row” means a row of type ITEM inside a BQ document (headers not counted).
  • Money. Totals by status exclude, or flag, the one FASA_DRAF row with a stored rate of RM 1,484,384.57 against a library rate of RM 120.70.
  • Charts. Hand-built SVG generated by charts.py straight from the CSVs; derived figures (percentages, outlier-free sums) are computed there and saved to charts/derived.json.

How sure is each statement

VerifiedProven by a file:line in the code or by a query on the snapshot in this round.
InferenceA reading of the facts, or of usual panel-contract practice. The owner must confirm it.
Needs your answerA decision or a fact only MPS staff can supply (chapter 90).

Privacy: this repository is public

  • Aggregates only: counts, sums, distributions, durations.
  • No staff names or usernames: staff appear as “PJA 1…12”.
  • No company names: companies appear as “Syarikat A…” or as anonymous columns.
  • No phone numbers, e-mail or IC numbers, no aduan ticket numbers.
  • Security and authentication are out of scope by the owner’s instruction and are not discussed.
  • Raw, un-sanitised query output stays in each sub-Yiyi’s raw/ folder and is not committed.

The final report.html was scanned against the staff and company name lists from the snapshot (work/bq/raw/privacy_scan.py); the raw output is in REPORT-pdf.md.

Known gaps

  • The findings call two votes over allocation; the CSV shows three on committed value (chapter 04).
  • Line totals: the 15 charted code areas sum to 25,055 lines; the findings quote ~25,500.
