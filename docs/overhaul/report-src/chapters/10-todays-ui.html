Today’s UI, honestly

Sixteen issues from a walk through the live build, ranked by harm. The worst four share one cause: no screen shows which contract a price belongs to.

1S1Nothing says where a rate comes from

“Kadar 510” stands alone: no rate book, no contract, no year. Inserting a template re-reads the library at that moment; after that the project keeps a frozen copy that records only source ids, never the rate book, the date or the original rate. pages/BQEditor.tsx:598-615 Verified

2S1No warning when the library moves

The amount has no “changed since” badge. When the PT edits a rate in Pustaka for a new contract, old projects silently keep the old rate and newly inserted templates silently take the new one. Two projects in the same month can price the same item differently, and nothing on screen shows it.
