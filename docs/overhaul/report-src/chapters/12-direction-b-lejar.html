UI/UX direction B · ledger-first

Lejar

InfraHub is a register of contracts and money, so it should read like one: calm paper, a serif masthead, dense ruled tables, and a BQ that is a real sheet.

The idea

Footnote-style “perlu perhatian” notes replace cards. The BQ editor becomes a sheet with column letters, row numbers and a formula bar that shows where the rate came from, plus a rate-book side panel: pick the book to compare against, see the item’s rate history across versions, decide kekal or guna baharu, write a reason.

Type and colour

Paper #F6F3EC, ink and ruled lines. MPS purple #3B2E8C marks anything contractual, green means kekal / ok, brick red a rate difference, pale yellow a highlighted row. MPS purple becomes the institutional anchor, as in the KIK report.

What to look at

  1. The contract/version tag on every register row, with SH/302/25·v2 flagged against v3.
  2. The BQ sheet: Kadar projek / Kadar buku / Beza columns and the rate-history side panel.
  3. The contract register with the version diff and the Klon kontrak form.

Pros

  • Strongest answer to “where does this rate come from”: every number has a source and a history.
  • Matches the documents InfraHub prints (BQ, CPC): screen and paper look like the same thing.
  • Highest information density on a desk; fewer clicks for the PT.

Cons

  • Weakest on a phone: the sheet needs a separate mobile measuring view (could borrow C’s).
  • Spreadsheet behaviour (keyboard navigation, frozen rows, fill-down) is the most engineering work of any screen in the three.
  • Serif and ledger may feel “old” to younger PJA if pushed into their screens.

Serves best

The PT and the Jurutera at a desk: checking, signing, reconciling BQ against Pelarasan and claims, printing.

Effort · Yiyi’s estimate, not measured

About 7–9 developer-weeks of UI. The BQ sheet is the big item: it replaces the wizard/accordion editor in pages/BQEditor.tsx (~2,000 lines).

Answers audit issues

1, 2, 3, 4 (source, history and diff on every row) and 10 (a real sheet instead of a wizard).
