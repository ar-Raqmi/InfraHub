How panel-contract maintenance work normally runs, mapped onto what InfraHub stores. Once the real object is clear, the fix almost designs itself.

How it normally works

Inference

Our reading of usual council practice. Please correct anything that differs at MPS (questions 1–6 in chapter 90).

Once a period, normally a year, the council runs one sebutharga to appoint a panel of contractors. The sebutharga document carries a Jadual Kadar Harga: a long list of work items, each with a unit and a fixed rate. Every panel contractor accepts that same schedule for the life of the contract.

After that, each complaint or job becomes a work order under the contract. The PJA measures the site and multiplies quantities by the schedule’s rates (the BQ). The PT appoints a panel contractor by rotation and raises the inden and BPP. The work is done, the quantities are re-measured (BQ Pelarasan), and payment is made at the same rates, never above the ordered amount.

FINDINGS-bq “What a master BQ really is”

A project’s rates are decided by the contract its work order was issued under, not by today’s date.

The rule this report rests on · a 2026 work order measured and paid in March 2027 still uses the 2026 schedule, including items added during pelarasan

The first contract change

The PT has two moves today, and both are unsafe

“The site’s master BQ” and “this contract’s schedule” have been the same thing because there has only ever been one contract. They stop being the same thing on the first day of the second contract. Here is what the PT could do that day. Verified mechanics Hypothetical scenario

Move 1 · overwrite

  • Closed projects are fine: their rows are copies.
  • Every open project that gets one more item, a template or a pelarasan addition now mixes old and new rates, with no warning.
  • The 2026 schedule no longer exists anywhere in the system. Re-adding an item at the old rate means typing the rate by hand.

Move 2 · duplicate

  • A second set of groups for the new contract sits next to the old one.
  • The picker shows both; nothing stops a PJA choosing the wrong year’s item.
  • The library doubles every year.

What the code and data already support Verified

  • The BQ PDF cites the Jawatankuasa Sebutharga meeting and says the company was chosen “dengan rotasi” (BQPDFExporter.ts:191).
  • Each year has its own list of sebutharga numbers, panel companies and votes; 2026 has exactly one number and 60 companies.
  • One rate per item across all companies. Pelarasan is capped at the contract price, and the standard opening bill says “ALL QUANTITY ARE PROVISIONAL” (BQEditor.tsx:583).
  • The 2026 panel was approved at a meeting dated 9 Disember 2025. The next panel contract may be only weeks away. Inference on timing
