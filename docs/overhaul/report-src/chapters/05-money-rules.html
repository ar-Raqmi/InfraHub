Money rules

Six formulas, all living inside the form

Every ringgit figure InfraHub shows comes from one of these rules. They work, and the stored numbers reconcile, but they are computed in the browser while the project page is open, and some are typed in more than one place.

Kos Projek (Harga Kontrak)

kos_projek = sum(amount of every row in bq_data)

The contract price is simply the BQ total. It is rewritten whenever the BQ changes, in any status.

LAD (late finish)

days = max(0, Siap Sebenar − Tamat Kontrak)
rate = sum < 20,000 ? RM 20 / day
      : sum × 6.4% ÷ 365

The constants exist in services/finance.ts, but the form types 0.064 and 20000 again inline. Two copies of one rule.

LoC (late claim)

LoC = max(0, (Tuntutan − Siap) − 14) × RM 100

Active unless the switch is off. RM 100 and 14 days are hard-coded in three files. The switch is 0 on every row, yet one project carries a RM 2,100 LoC.

Kos Sebenar (the cap)

kos_sebenar = max(0, min(Pelarasan, Kos Projek)
          − LAD − LoC − Wang Tahanan)

Payment never exceeds the ordered amount. Pelarasan is the sum of bq_data_pelarasan, or Kos Projek if there is none yet.

Overflow (bq_pelarasan_extra)

extra = max(0, Pelarasan − Kos Projek)

What the re-measurement found above the contract price. It is stored, then removed from payment by the cap. Nobody is told.

Where the rules live, and why it matters

Inside React effects

The form has 26 useState and 20 useEffect. Results are written back into the record and saved. With no tests, a change to LAD or the cap can only be checked by reading code. Verified

In more than one place

LAD constants twice, LoC constants in three files, Harga Akhir re-written in the list page. Two screens can disagree after a policy change. Verified

Some are only reported

The RM 200,000 company cap is printed on the Senarai PDF but not enforced; one company is RM 50.67 over. The vote balance changes with the on-screen filters. Verified
